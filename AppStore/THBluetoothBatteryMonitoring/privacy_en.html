Privacy Policy

BatteryMonitor

Last Updated: July 7, 2026

1. Overview

BatteryMonitor (the "App") helps you monitor Bluetooth peripheral battery levels, connection status, local battery history, widgets and battery-related notifications.

Core commitment: The App does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs or developer-operated tracking servers. Bluetooth and battery data is used to provide App features and is stored locally on your device or, when iCloud is available, in Apple-managed iCloud storage for sync.

2. Information We Process

To provide the App's core functions, the App may process and store the following information:

  • Bluetooth peripheral identifiers exposed by iOS Core Bluetooth, device names, custom names and device type selections;
  • Battery percentage, charging state, multi-component battery values, connection state, read status and last-seen timestamps;
  • Battery reading history used for charts, statistics, prediction and notification decisions;
  • Notification settings, quiet hours, low-battery thresholds, refresh strategy, data retention preference and theme preference;
  • Widget display data, including selected device status and predicted battery timeline entries;
  • StoreKit purchase entitlement status for Lifetime Pro.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not intentionally collect or transmit:

  • Name, email address, phone number, contacts, calendar data or precise location;
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking identifiers;
  • Browsing history, microphone data, camera data, photos or files;
  • Third-party analytics events, advertising profiles or behavioral tracking data.

4. Bluetooth and Device Data

The App uses iOS Core Bluetooth to scan for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy peripherals, connect to devices you choose, read supported battery services or supported vendor-specific battery characteristics, and update saved device state.

Bluetooth access may continue in the background when iOS allows it so the App can refresh battery status and send battery alerts. The App does not use Bluetooth data to infer your location and does not sell or share Bluetooth identifiers for advertising.

5. Local Storage, Widgets and iCloud

The App stores device records and battery readings in Core Data. App settings are stored in iOS UserDefaults. Widget data may be written to an App Group shared container so the iOS widget can display device status and predicted battery timelines.

The App is configured with Apple's CloudKit-backed Core Data sync. If iCloud is available for your Apple ID and the feature is enabled by the system, device and battery records may sync through Apple's iCloud infrastructure. The developer does not operate a separate sync server and cannot directly access your iCloud account data.

6. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App may schedule local notifications for low battery, predicted rapid battery drain, charging completion, connection and disconnection events. Notification preferences and recent alert timestamps are stored locally to respect your settings and avoid repeated alerts.

7. Purchases

The App may offer a Lifetime Pro purchase through Apple StoreKit. Purchase, restoration and entitlement validation are handled by Apple StoreKit. The App stores only the resulting local entitlement state needed to unlock App features. Payment information is processed by Apple, not by the developer.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Battery history retention follows the retention option you choose in the App, such as 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 1 year or forever. You may remove saved devices, clear local data through available App controls, disable iCloud sync in system settings, revoke Bluetooth or notification permissions, or uninstall the App to remove sandboxed local App data.

9. Third-Party Services

The App relies on Apple system frameworks and services, including Core Bluetooth, Core Data, CloudKit, WidgetKit, UserNotifications and StoreKit. These services are governed by Apple's privacy practices and your Apple ID/iCloud settings. The App does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics or crash reporting SDKs.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general utility use and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not provide developer-operated accounts or servers, guardians can remove local App data by deleting saved devices or uninstalling the App.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App features, platform requirements or legal requirements change. Updated versions may be published on this page, in App release notes or inside the App.
