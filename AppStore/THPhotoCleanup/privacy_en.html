Privacy Policy

Photo Cleanup · 拾光

Effective and last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Scope and developer

This Privacy Policy applies to Photo Cleanup (also called “拾光” or “Shiguang”, the “App”), provided by developer Tan (“we”, “us”). The App helps organize photos and videos, find duplicate or similar media, flag potentially sensitive photos, compress videos, and create still versions of Live Photos.

2. Overview

The App requires no account and includes no advertising, behavioral analytics, or third-party crash reporting SDKs. We do not track you across apps or sell or rent personal information. Media analysis, text recognition, and processing run on your device. We operate no server that receives your photos, videos, or analysis records. System services may still use the network, including iCloud media downloads and Photos synchronization, Apple place-name lookup, and App Store purchases and restoration.

3. Photo access and on-device analysis

With your permission, the App accesses available photos, videos, thumbnails, and information such as media identifiers, capture dates, dimensions, duration, favorites, albums, and existing photo coordinates. These support previews, classification, similarity analysis, duplicate verification, size calculation, and organization suggestions. Limited Photos access restricts the available library; full scanning and certain album features require full access.

Apple Vision performs visual and text recognition on-device. This can process sensitive text in identity documents, bank cards, contracts, or other images to flag photos for your review. Full recognized text is not saved as a text archive or uploaded; classification flags and other analysis results are stored locally. This is not identity verification and cannot detect every sensitive item.

4. Photo locations and Apple place lookup

The App groups places and possible trips using existing photo coordinates and capture times, without requesting live device location. When organization results refresh, place-name resolution may automatically send representative coordinates from some photos to Apple through CLGeocoder to obtain city or region names. These can be precise coordinates; they are not guaranteed to be blurred. Photo images and recognized text are not included in these requests.

Limiting Photos access reduces the available media and metadata; revoking access prevents further photo access. There is currently no separate switch for place-name lookup. The Wi-Fi-only setting for cloud originals governs media downloads, not all system network requests. Apple handles these requests under its Privacy Policy.

5. iCloud, saving, and deletion

If originals are stored only in iCloud, the system Photos service may download them according to network conditions and App settings for verification, size calculation, or processing. Saving, album changes, and deletions you confirm may synchronize to iCloud and other devices according to your iCloud Photos settings. Apple provides this synchronization.

Video compression and Live Photo still creation save new media. Deleting original media requires separate confirmation. Saved results may retain capture dates and locations; the App does not guarantee removal of all metadata. Deleting the App neither reverses completed photo deletions nor removes media saved to Photos.

6. Local storage and retention

The App stores preferences, protected album settings, selections and review progress, ignored suggestions, operation history, media identifiers, analysis caches, and free-usage counters on your device. Processing may also create temporary videos or images. These records support continued organization, avoid repeat analysis, show history, and apply free-use allowances.

The state and analysis-cache directory is excluded from system backup. Backup of other local data depends on system settings. Records and caches may remain until updated, cleared, or removed with the App. Temporary files are removed after processing or system cleanup, although interrupted operations may leave residual files. Deleting the App, rather than offloading it while retaining data, generally removes sandbox records. Manage saved media separately in Photos.

7. Purchases and Apple services

Photo Cleanup Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase through Apple StoreKit, with no subscription or automatic renewal. The App reads Apple-verified transactions and entitlements to unlock features, restore purchases, and reflect revocations or refunds. Apple handles billing, Apple Account, and payment information; we do not receive card details or your Apple Account password. Apple may retain transaction records under its policies; deleting the App does not erase those records.

8. Support and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, follow up, and meet necessary obligations, retaining them only as needed for those purposes. You may request deletion, subject to legally required retention. Please do not send original identity documents, bank-card images, or unnecessary personal information.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages without added analytics scripts or advertisements. GitHub may process request logs such as IP addresses when you visit. Submissions to GitHub Issues may be public. These services are subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Your choices and rights

You can change or revoke access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos, adjust organization preferences, restore ignored suggestions, and delete the App to remove local records. Restoring suggestions does not reset free-use counters. Contact us for access, correction, deletion, or other applicable data-rights requests. We cannot remotely access or erase media and records held only on your device. Revoking permission does not undo completed operations.

10. Security and children

The App relies on iOS permissions, sandboxing, and system file protection. No system guarantees absolute security. Protect your device credentials and back up important media. The App is not specifically directed at children. Minors should use it with guardian guidance and obtain guardian permission for purchases or important deletions. Contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal information.

11. Policy changes

We may update this policy and its date when features or processing practices change. Material changes will be explained in the App, release notes, or this page. We will obtain any additional permission or consent required by law rather than treating continued use as a substitute.
