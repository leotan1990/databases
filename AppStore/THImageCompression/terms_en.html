6. Image credits

Each pack adds 100 credits, with no time-based expiry. Permanent access, an active Pro subscription or trial, and an active pass take priority over credits; credits are not used while that access is active.

Without active unlimited access, one credit is used when an image first produces a usable compressed result in that task. Batch tasks are settled per image. A usable result is not a guarantee that every target size is reached; the interface reports that separately. Import failure, compression failure and cancellation do not use credits. If an attempt fails, its first successful retry uses one credit. Retrying the same result, retrying a failed save, sharing and re-exporting from history do not charge again. Credit use depends on producing a usable result, not on whether a later save to Photos or Files succeeds.

The credit wallet requires iOS 16 or later and a verifiable original App Store purchase identity. Buying and using credits also requires an available iCloud private ledger. Pro purchases remain available on iOS 15.6, and verified original paid-download customers retain lifetime access. If confirmation is incomplete, follow the sync or restore instructions.

A refund or revocation removes the corresponding credit grant. Credits already used remain in the usage record and may create a credit shortfall. Future top-ups first cover that shortfall before increasing the spendable balance. For example, buying a 100-credit pack with an existing 50-credit shortfall increases the available balance by 50 credits. This ledger adjustment does not automatically charge an additional payment.
