BeatCut Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current BeatCut iOS app, version 1.0. Imported media is processed on your device. BeatCut has no account system, advertising, subscription or in-app purchase.

Effective and last updated: October 26, 2026

Privacy · Terms · Support

1. Developer and contact

BeatCut is provided under the developer’s public name Huang. This policy covers BeatCut 1.0 and these support pages. For privacy questions and support, email vivi.huang.2026@foxmail.com.

2. Your media stays under your control

BeatCut copies the photos, videos and audio files you select into its app storage so you can reopen a project. It analyzes audio, creates edit plans and renders videos on your device. The current app does not transmit your media, project contents, usage events or identifiers to developer servers. It does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

3. Permissions and system services

The system photo and document pickers provide the items you choose; BeatCut does not request access to your entire photo library. When you choose Save to Photos, the app requests permission to add the exported video. You can refuse that permission and still export or share a file. Apple or a file provider may download an item stored in iCloud or another service when you select it. Those services follow their own settings and policies.

4. Storage, deletion and backups

Project copies, edit plans and retained exports are stored in the app container. The app also keeps reusable media and audio-analysis caches and local render diagnostics, such as duration, file size, OS version and thermal state. These diagnostics are not automatically sent to the developer. Deleting a project removes that project's copies and exports, but does not remove your originals, videos saved to Photos, files shared elsewhere, reusable caches or an older migration backup. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app storage. Existing device backups and exported copies are managed through the relevant system or service.

5. Sharing and requesting support

When you choose an action in the system share sheet, the selected exported video is handed to the app or service you choose. BeatCut does not choose a recipient or send it automatically. If you contact support by email, the operator receives the information you choose to send and uses it to respond and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending private media or other people's information unless it is necessary. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request, meet applicable legal obligations or resolve a related dispute. Our email provider processes messages to deliver and store that correspondence.

6. Website hosting

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. GitHub handles website requests under its own privacy statement. These pages do not add advertising, analytics, forms, third-party fonts or tracking scripts. This website activity is separate from BeatCut’s on-device media processing.

GitHub General Privacy Statement

7. Children and privacy requests

BeatCut is a general-purpose editing tool, not a service directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. If personal information was sent to support, please contact us so we can address it. You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information in support correspondence, subject to applicable law. We cannot access or delete copies held in your device backups or other apps and services.

8. Your choices and policy updates

You can manage Photos permissions in iOS Settings, delete individual projects, remove exported copies in the destination service, or delete the app's local data by deleting the app. There is no BeatCut account to delete. Contact the operator about support correspondence or privacy questions. If app behavior or hosting changes, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be reviewed and updated before release.
