BeatCut Help & Support

Free · No in-app purchases · No subscriptions

Effective and last updated: October 26, 2026

Privacy · Terms · Support

1. Make your first video

Start a new project and select 5–30 photos or videos. Import a local, readable audio file that covers your chosen 15, 30 or 60 seconds. Adjust material order, choose Chill, Dynamic or Beat, and choose a portrait, square or landscape frame. Generate a preview, then export 1080p or 4K. A successful export is retained in the project.

2. Adjust the edit

Drag the handles in Reorder to change material order. Tap a material to adjust its framing or a video's source range. Open Listen and select to choose a different music starting point and hear the selected interval. Try another version creates a different edit; returning to edit keeps your project.

3. The rhythm is not obvious

Beat detection works best when the music has a clear rhythm. Complex or very weak rhythms may produce fewer useful beat positions. When confidence is low, BeatCut explains that it uses evenly spaced cuts. Material count and length can also prevent some cuts from landing on beats; the preview shows a notice. Audio longer than 30 minutes is not analyzed for beats.

4. A music file will not import

Use a file you can access through Files, rather than a protected streaming-library item. Download cloud files first and check that the audio plays. A protected, damaged or unsupported audio track cannot be used. Choose music and imagery you have permission to use.

5. Export, save and interruptions

Keep BeatCut in the foreground while generating or exporting. Moving it to the background cancels the current task so it can finish cleanup; return to the app and retry. For export failures, free device storage and try 1080p. 4K availability and speed depend on the device and source media. If saving to Photos is denied, change the permission in Settings or use Share file. If an export succeeds but the app says it could not retain it in the project, use Save to Photos or Share file before leaving the export screen.

6. Find your projects and exports

Projects are stored on this device. Open a project from the home screen to continue editing, or choose View videos from its menu to open retained exports. Videos saved to Photos or shared to Files remain in those destinations. Removing BeatCut without a usable backup may lose local projects.
