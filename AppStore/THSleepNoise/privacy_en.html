Privacy Policy

Aura Sleep (Aura Sleep: White Noise & Rain)

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

1. Overview & Core Commitment

Welcome to Aura Sleep ("the App"). The App is developed and operated by an independent developer and provides ambient sound mixing and sound-creation tools.

Our Core Privacy Promise: The App does not contain third-party advertising SDKs or behavioral tracking and analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your personal data. Your audio and mix settings are processed and stored locally by the App, subject to the sharing and system-backup choices described below. If you choose to send feedback, the feedback data described in Section 2 is sent to the developer through Apple CloudKit.

2. System Permissions & Data Processing

The App processes audio on your device and requests microphone permission only for recording. Sending feedback is a separate, voluntary action.

  • Microphone Access (Optional): When you start a recording, the App requests microphone permission. Recorded audio is processed and saved in the App's local storage. Denying microphone access prevents recording but does not affect other playback and mixing features.
  • Local Audio, Sharing & Backups: Recordings, imported audio, generated sounds, mixes, and completed export files are processed and stored locally. The App does not automatically upload these audio files or your mix settings to the developer or the feedback database. If you choose to export or share a file, it is sent to the destination you select through the system sharing interface, and that destination's practices apply. Files and preferences may also be included in backups managed by iOS or your device/account settings; those backups are not controlled by the App.
  • Audio Playback & Background Audio: The App uses Apple's AVFoundation framework for playback, including background and lock-screen playback. It does not send your listening history, recordings, or mixes to the developer during playback.

Voluntary In-App Feedback

Only when you tap the feedback submission button does the App send the following to the developer through Apple CloudKit:

  • The feedback text you enter;
  • An optional reply email address, if you enter one;
  • The App version and build number, iOS version, and current language/region setting, to provide technical context for your feedback.

An iCloud account is required to submit feedback. CloudKit stores the feedback in the App's CloudKit container and automatically maintains record metadata, including creation information and an app-scoped creator user identifier. This identifier associates the record with the account that created it for CloudKit record ownership and permissions. It is not an advertising identifier. The App does not read your Apple ID email address, real name, or Apple ID credentials; the reply email sent with feedback is only the one you choose to type into the form.

The submitted information is used to handle and respond to your feedback and diagnose related technical issues. Your optional email is used only to reply to your feedback. Feedback records and their metadata are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. CloudKit is hosted by Apple, and record access is governed by the container's CloudKit permissions. Submitted feedback is not an anonymous record because it can be associated with its CloudKit creator and any email you provide.

3. Local Storage & System API Declarations

In accordance with Apple's Privacy Manifest guidelines, the App declares the following system API categories:

  • User Defaults (UserDefaults - CA92.1): Used to save local mix configuration, sleep timer preferences, favorites, and other App preferences. Favorite scene information may also be shared on the device with the App's widget through its App Group. The App does not upload these preferences to its feedback database; system-managed backups depend on your device/account settings.
  • File Timestamp (FileTimestamp - C617.1): Used within the App container to organize and manage custom audio and completed export files.
  • Elapsed-Time Timers (SystemBootTime - 35F9.1): Used locally to measure elapsed time for audio previews and playback transitions. The App does not send system boot time to the developer.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

The App provides optional "Aura Pro" access through Apple's StoreKit system, with monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a lifetime one-time purchase. Current prices, billing periods, and any introductory offers available to your account are shown on the App Store purchase page. A free trial is available only when you are eligible and the purchase page shows an available trial offer.

  • All financial transactions, billing processing, and subscription renewals are managed solely and securely by Apple Inc. via the App Store.
  • The developer has no access to and never stores your credit card details, payment accounts, or Apple ID credentials.
  • The App reads locally verified App Store transaction status on your device to determine Aura Pro access. Basic mixing does not require Aura Pro. Access to the full Pro sound library and creating new AAC audio exports require Aura Pro; sharing an already completed export does not require a currently active membership.

5. Information We Do Not Request or Track

Other than the voluntary feedback information described in Section 2, the App does not request or automatically extract the following for collection by the developer:

  • Your real name, phone number, government ID, or Apple ID credentials;
  • Your contacts, address book, calendar appointments, or personal notes;
  • Precise GPS location data or location history;
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking fingerprints;
  • Biometric data (Face ID, Touch ID) or clinical health records.

The optional email and app-scoped CloudKit creator identifier used with feedback are described above. If you voluntarily include other personal details in your feedback text, those details are sent with the feedback. Please include only what is necessary for your support request.

6. Data Retention & User Control

  • Local Audio and Exports: You can delete custom sounds and completed export files using the App's management pages. Files you have shared or saved outside the App are controlled separately by the destination you selected.
  • Managing Permissions: You can grant or revoke microphone permission through iOS Settings > Aura Sleep.
  • Removing the App: Deleting the App removes its local sandbox data from the device under iOS rules. This does not automatically remove copies in system backups, files saved elsewhere, or feedback previously submitted to CloudKit.
  • Feedback Data Requests: Feedback records remain in the App's CloudKit container unless handled or deleted there; uninstalling the App does not delete them. You can contact vivi.huang.2026@foxmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your submitted feedback. Please provide enough details to locate the submission, such as approximately when it was sent and the reply email you supplied, if any. The App does not implement a fixed automatic expiry period for feedback records.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-utility mindfulness and sleep aid tool and is not specifically directed toward children under 13 (or the legal threshold in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you are under the age of majority, please use this App under parental guidance.

Your continued use of the App following the posting of modifications indicates your agreement to the updated policy.
