Privacy Policy

Tangyuan: Unzip & Compress

Effective and last updated: August 9, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Tangyuan: Unzip & Compress (the “App”) handles information when you use it on an iPhone or iPad.

Our core principle: Your archives, extracted files, created ZIP files, and passwords are processed on your device. The App does not require an account, operate a developer-controlled upload service, serve advertising, include third-party analytics, or track you across apps or websites.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

The developer does not collect, store, sell, rent, or transmit the following to developer-operated servers:

  • archives, documents, folders, extracted content, or ZIP files you create;
  • archive passwords;
  • your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or account credentials;
  • precise location, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking data; or
  • usage analytics, behavioral profiles, or developer-collected crash logs.

The App has no developer-operated account or backend system, so the developer cannot remotely view or retrieve your local files.

2. Files and On-Device Processing

The App processes archive operations locally on your device. Depending on the action you choose:

  • files selected through Files, a document-opening flow, or the share extension are copied into an App-managed local workspace;
  • archive contents are read locally to inspect or extract supported formats;
  • selected files and folders are read locally to create ZIP or ZIP64 archives; and
  • results leave the App only when you deliberately export, save, preview, or share them through an Apple system interface or another app.

If you send a file to another app, cloud-storage provider, or sharing service, that recipient handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Review the destination before sharing.

3. Passwords

A password you enter to open or create an encrypted archive is held in memory only for the active task. The App does not save archive passwords to files, preferences, Keychain, or a remote service. A password is cleared from the relevant input after the task begins or the input is dismissed.

The developer cannot recover a forgotten archive password.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following locally:

  • Workspace files: imported archives, extracted folders, and ZIP files you create;
  • Temporary task data: local staging files used while an archive operation is in progress;
  • Trial status: the number of successful advanced trial tasks used, stored in the iOS Keychain; and
  • Purchase entitlement: the App checks signed App Store transaction information through Apple’s StoreKit framework to determine whether Pro access is active.

This local data is used only to provide the feature you requested, display your workspace, enforce the included trial, or restore your purchase status.

5. Purchases and Apple Services

The optional lifetime Pro purchase is offered through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the transaction and may handle information such as your Apple Account, payment details, purchase history, region, and transaction diagnostics under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

The App may connect to Apple’s App Store services to load product information, complete or restore a purchase, and verify the entitlement. Apple may also provide crash or diagnostic information according to your device and App Store privacy settings. The App does not add a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Workspace files remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Temporary processing data is normally removed after a task succeeds, fails, or is cancelled. Stale staging data is also cleaned automatically.
  • You can remove workspace files using the App’s delete controls.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its sandboxed workspace data under normal iOS behavior. Some Keychain data, including the trial counter, may persist after uninstall as permitted by iOS.

Because the developer does not hold a server-side user profile or uploaded files, there is no developer-hosted account or archive data for the developer to export or delete.

7. Permissions and Access

The App accesses only files you select or send to it through Apple’s document and sharing interfaces. You can stop sharing files with the App at any time by not selecting or sending additional files, and you can delete imported copies from the App workspace.

Denying or withdrawing system access may prevent the related import, preview, export, or sharing feature from working.

8. Security

Local files are protected by the iOS app sandbox and Apple platform security. The App also applies archive-safety checks during processing. However, no software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Use caution with archives from untrusted sources and keep backups of important files.

9. Local Third-Party Components

The App includes archive-processing components such as ZipArchive, libarchive, XZ Utils/liblzma, Zstandard, PLzmaSDK, and UnRAR. These components are used for local compression, decompression, and format support. The App does not use them to transmit your files or personal information to their authors. Applicable license notices are available in the App.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is a general-purpose file utility and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or provide public posting, messaging, or social features.

11. International Use

Since the developer does not receive your App files or operate an App account service, the developer does not transfer those files or account data between countries. Apple or a destination you choose when sharing may process information in other regions under its own policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features, data practices, legal obligations, or platform requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. If a change materially affects how the App handles data, we will provide an appropriate additional notice.
