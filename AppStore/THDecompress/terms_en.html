2. App Features

The App is an on-device archive utility for iPhone and iPad. Depending on the file, device, system version, and available access, it may allow you to:

  • extract ZIP/ZIPX, 7Z, RAR/RAR5, TAR, GZ, BZ2, XZ, ZST, and supported compressed-TAR formats;
  • open supported password-protected ZIP, 7Z, RAR4, and RAR5 archives;
  • create ZIP and ZIP64 archives, including AES-256 encrypted ZIP files;
  • import files through Files, document-opening flows, and the share extension; and
  • preview, manage, delete, export, and share files using the App workspace and Apple system interfaces.

Supported formats and capabilities may change as the App, Apple platforms, and third-party archive formats evolve. The App does not promise support for every archive variant, encryption method, multipart archive, damaged file, or third-party implementation.
